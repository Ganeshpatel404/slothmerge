04 · engineering honesty

        
Where open won. And where it cost me.


        

            

                
Where open won

                

                    Private code repositories are full of proprietary logic, database connection parameters, and unreleased IP. Sending that to external APIs isn't just expensive—in many companies, it's a direct compliance violation. With Gemma running on local open weights, privacy isn't a marketing checkbox: it is physically guaranteed by airgapping the process.
                

            


            

                
Where it cost me (the scars)

                

                    Small 2B open-weight models initially struggled with calculating accurate line numbers across multi-hunk diffs—they hallucinated line offsets. The fix was to stop asking the model to do arithmetic: I built a deterministic Python hunk parser that maps AST anchors, and let Gemma focus strictly on evaluating vulnerability semantics.
